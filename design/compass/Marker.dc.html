Marker housing — what holds the icon
Shipped size is a 20px plate carrying a 12px icon and a 7px badge, from 64×64 art. Each concept is shown at 4× and then at real size on the strip, twice, because two of these only fall apart when they sit next to each other.
A — DiamondSHIPPED
The rotated square wastes its own corners: a 20px diamond only fits a 14px inscribed square, so the 12px icon is already at the ceiling. Distinctive, and the tightest fit of the four.
B — PipREADS SMALLEST
A circle at 20px has no corners to lose, so the same icon breathes. But it is the WoW minimap blip shape — it stops reading as pfQuest, and it collides visually with tracking dots.
C — TagMOST ROOM
The notch points at the heading rather than merely sitting above it, and a square body fits a 14px icon in the same 20px. Reads as a map tag, which is what it is.
D — BareNO ART AT ALL
Drop the housing: icon plus a 1px underline. The icon gets the whole 20px, three textures stop shipping, and the strip goes quiet. Loses the type-colour cue the plate edge carries today.
The shipped art, at 4×
marker_fill and marker_edge, 64×64 each, drawn white so SetVertexColor can tint them to whatever the theme accent is. Any replacement keeps that rule — white art, tinted at runtime — or the GW2 gold and standalone teal both stop working from one file.
